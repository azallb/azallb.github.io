I am honoured to announce that my first paper, written with L. Varnet (found [here in PDF](http://azallb.github.io/files/Ahumada-study_lebagousse_varnet2025.pdf) and [published on HAL](https://hal.science/hal-05060148)), was nominated by the SFA (French Society of Acoustics) as one of the 6 best papers written by all PhD/Masters students for the 17th edition of the French Congress for Acoustics (CFA 2025), out of all the conference papers submitted for the congress ! I am beyond grateful for this nomination and for L. Varnet's supervision on this project.
